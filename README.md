# Grigoriy Voyakin

### Applied AI Engineer · Multimodal AI Systems · Generative Media

I build practical AI systems across generative image and video, computer vision, LLM/VLM applications, model evaluation, automation, and human-in-the-loop workflows.

My background combines software engineering, AI product development, technical leadership, and more than seven years in film and advertising production. I have led teams and technical initiatives, worked directly with clients and cross-functional stakeholders, and taken ownership of complex projects from ambiguous requirements to working solutions.

## Current Focus

I continuously deepen my work across the full applied AI lifecycle: model training and adaptation, controlled experimentation, evaluation, inference, product integration, and human-supervised automation.

My current direction is toward reliable multimodal AI systems that connect generative models, computer vision, LLM/VLM applications, agents, and reusable evaluation infrastructure. I am particularly focused on making model quality, failure modes, evidence, latency, cost, and human decision-making part of one coherent engineering process.

## Selected Work

- **[Human-in-the-Loop Generative ML Lab](https://github.com/Grigoriy-V/human-in-the-loop-generative-ml-lab)**  
  Generative model training, latent SiT, REPA, controlled evaluation, experiment tracking, and human-supervised agent orchestration.

- **[Visual Quality Gate](https://github.com/Grigoriy-V/visual-quality-gate)**  
  A runnable multimodal review system for structured visual QA, guide compliance, safe verdicts, evaluation, and cost tracking.

- **[Geni AI](https://github.com/Grigoriy-V/geni-ai-telegram-miniapp)**  
  An end-to-end generative AI product covering frontend, AI workflows, automation, payments, infrastructure, telemetry, and product analytics.
